From the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Baby Care app and log in.
  2. Tap More → Settings & Privacy.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE (or "মুছে ফেলুন") and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email smnr7770@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Baby Care account deletion". After verifying the request we delete the account within 7 days (30 days at most) and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, email, photos and addresses
  • Your children's information and photos
  • Your posts, comments, reactions and saved content
  • Your points, gift redemptions and contest entries
  • Your complaints, notifications and settings

What is kept

For accounting and legal reasons we keep order records (products, quantities, amounts and dates) with your name, address and phone number removed. Data is removed from server backups within 30 days.
